* Chapter 8 — The Algebra Behind FRI Folding

Introduce

$$ f(X)=g(X^2)+Xh(X^2). $$

Then derive

$$ g(x^2)=\frac{f(x)+f(-x)}2 $$

and

$$ h(x^2)=\frac{f(x)-f(-x)}{2x}. $$

Then explain how the protocol uses a random challenge to combine these pieces into a new lower-degree polynomial.

This is where students can finally see why FRI can repeatedly reduce the degree.
