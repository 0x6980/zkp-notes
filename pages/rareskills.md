### RareSkills — Technical Writing & Research Contribution

**Co-author / Technical Contributor — Number Theoretic Transform (NTT)**

Contributed to the development of educational and technical material on the **Number Theoretic Transform (NTT)** as part of the RareSkills Book of Zero Knowledge.

The work focuses on developing an intuitive yet mathematically grounded understanding of NTT, explaining its underlying algebraic structure and computational principles rather than merely presenting the algorithm.

Topics covered include:

- **Finite Field Algebra:** Multiplicative subgroups, cyclic groups, roots of unity, and their role in polynomial evaluation.
- **NTT Fundamentals:** The mathematical foundations of the Number Theoretic Transform and its relationship to the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT).
- **Polynomial Evaluation:** Vandermonde matrices, structured evaluation domains, and the algebraic properties that enable efficient evaluation.
- **Image Preservation:** Understanding how polynomial evaluations can be reused across structured domains, providing the mathematical intuition behind NTT.
- **Inverse NTT (INTT):** Recovering polynomial coefficients from evaluation representations.
- **Fast Polynomial Arithmetic:** The convolution theorem and the use of NTT/INTT for efficient polynomial multiplication.
- **Connection to Zero-Knowledge Proofs:** Exploring the structural relationship between NTT and FRI (Fast Reed–Solomon Interactive Oracle Proof of Proximity).

The material builds the mathematical concepts incrementally, connecting abstract algebraic properties to practical algorithms used in modern cryptographic systems.
